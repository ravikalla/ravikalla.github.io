🎯 How to Play

Hunt for letters to spell words! Read the hint, then click on letters to reveal the hidden word. Complete all words to master the forest!

🌲 Game Features

• 10 different words to discover

• Helpful hints with emojis for each word

• Progressive difficulty from simple to complex words

• Visual feedback for correct and incorrect letters

🏆 Scoring

• 10 points for each correct letter

• 50 bonus points for completing a word

• Level progression based on words completed

⌨️ Controls

• Click on letter buttons to guess letters

• Green buttons show correct letters

• Red buttons show incorrect guesses

• Use Tab to navigate between letter buttons

• Press Enter or Space to select letters

• Press Escape to close this help
